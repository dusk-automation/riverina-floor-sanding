Foot traffic wears down the lustre of a floor over the years, and the busier the room, the duller it gets. Often the floor does not need a full re-sand: a light sand and new top coats revive it, and make it quicker and easier to clean.

We are happy to look at your floor in person and tell you what treatment it needs.

Sound advice

What a sand and polish will not do.

We would rather tell you now than surprise you later. A professionally sanded floor looks beautiful, but please do not expect:

  • A finish with no dust in it at all. No home is a clean room, so a few specks can settle in a wet coat. That is why cars are painted in a spray booth.
  • Deep cuts and gouges to disappear. Carpet knife cuts and deep gouges usually sit below what a sand can reach.
  • Stains inside the boards to sand out. Pet stains and water marks around nail holes can stay.
  • Gaps between boards to be filled. Timber shrinks and swells through the year, and filler in the gaps cracks.
  • The timber to stay still. Boards move, and we cannot take responsibility for later movement that changes how the floor looks.
  • Borer-damaged boards to match. They will not have the same gloss or the same smooth feel as sound boards.
  • Putty to feel like timber. Filled spots feel a little different once the coats go on.
